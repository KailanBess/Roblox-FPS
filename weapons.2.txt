87. WORKSHOP SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The Workshop is the player creativity foundation of the FPS ecosystem.

The purpose of the Workshop is to allow players to:

Design weapons
Create unique visual identities
Share creations
Participate in the game's content ecosystem
Build a creator reputation

The Workshop transforms players from only consumers into contributors.

87.1 Workshop Philosophy

The Workshop follows three principles:

Creativity Without Competitive Chaos

Players can create unlimited designs.

However:

Ranked gameplay requires balance.

Expression Without Pay-To-Win

Workshop weapons can be unique.

They cannot become unfair.

Community Driven Content

The best creations should become visible.
