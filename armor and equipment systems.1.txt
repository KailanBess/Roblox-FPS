122. ARMOR SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Armor is a defensive customization system designed to provide:

Build identity
Strategic choices
Long-term progression
Cosmetic expression

Armor does not replace player skill.

Armor does not create large power gaps.

Armor provides controlled defensive customization through:

Damage reduction
Affixes
Set bonuses
Cosmetic customization
122.1 ARMOR DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Armor follows three principles:

1. Defense Through Choice

Players customize how they approach combat.

Examples:

A player may choose:

More mobility
Better reload
Better weapon specialization
2. Controlled Advantage

Armor bonuses must remain small.

Armor cannot:

Create unbeatable builds
Break TTK
Replace gun skill
3. Build Identity

Armor should allow:

"How do I want to play?"

not:

"How do I become stronger than everyone?"

30%

132. ARMOR AFFIX BALANCE RECOMMENDATIONS

Additional balance rules:

Damage Affixes

Should be low impact.

Reason:

Damage directly affects TTK.

Fire Rate Affixes

Require careful control.

Reason:

Fire rate affects DPS.

Accuracy Affixes

Can be slightly stronger.

Reason:

Improves consistency, not raw power.

Movement Affixes

Limited.

Reason:

Movement affects every fight.

Armor Pen Affixes

Limited heavily.

Reason:

Counters the defensive system.
