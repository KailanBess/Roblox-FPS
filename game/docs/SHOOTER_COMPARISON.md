# Roblox shooter design comparisons

This is a design comparison using broadly known gameplay patterns, not a live
review of current updates, shops, prices, device availability or monetization.
The environment's request to the official RIVALS Roblox page was blocked by its
network proxy (CONNECT 403). Domain additions for Roblox pages/API/docs were saved
to the environment draft; they must be applied before a current-source audit.
No claim is made that the pages below were successfully read during this task.
No competitor is being labeled pay-to-win.

| Reference | Design pattern to learn from | Direction for this game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [RIVALS](https://www.roblox.com/games/17625359962/RIVALS) | Focused duels, readable rounds and immediately understandable combat goals | Keep combat feedback and round objectives clear; differentiate through optional player-created weapon appearance and a published fairness contract |
| [Arsenal](https://www.roblox.com/games/286090429/Arsenal) | Quick onboarding and weapon variety through rotation | Make a first match work with finished weapons; creation should be an extra path, not required homework |
| [Phantom Forces](https://www.roblox.com/games/292439477/Phantom-Forces) | Distinct weapon roles, detailed loadout decisions and range-dependent fights | Show tradeoffs and stat explanations, but keep free combat access and small bonuses instead of overwhelming newcomers with progression power |
| [Bad Business](https://www.roblox.com/games/3233893879/Bad-Business) | Broad weapon personalization and expressive loadouts | Make the player's authored voxel design the centerpiece; keep movement supportive rather than building the identity around traversal mastery |
| [FRONTLINES](https://www.roblox.com/games/5938036553/FRONTLINES) | Strong weapon presentation, audio/animation and polished first-person feedback | Invest in feel and readable presentation while keeping voxel art and low-end device performance |

The useful differentiator is not a larger gun count. It is a simple promise the
player can verify: all combat templates and creation tools are free, weapon stats
are visible, opponents follow the same power budget, and purchases do not improve
combat. Let competitive players ignore creation and let creators build identity
without needing to buy power.

## Verify with current sources before marketing comparisons

Read the official experience pages, current developer announcements and storefronts
once network access is enabled. Check controls/device availability on actual
hardware, and record observation dates. Do not infer current monetization or
competitive fairness from screenshots, search snippets or old reputation. A proper
current comparison would inspect actual player acquisition, unlock time, premium
currency conversion, exclusive gameplay items and paid progression—not assume
that cosmetic-looking stores are automatically fair or unfair.
