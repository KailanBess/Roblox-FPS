19. DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

These rules apply to all future development.

Constraint 1 — No Pay To Win

Purchases cannot provide:

Combat advantage
Exclusive power
Better stats
Constraint 2 — Competitive Balance

All weapons must respect:

MAX TTK VARIANCE ±25%

CLASS DPS VARIANCE ±15%
Constraint 3 — Customization Limits

Customization cannot break:

Weapon identity
Competitive integrity
Match fairness
Constraint 4 — Economy Stability

Avoid:

Excessive inflation
Unlimited currency generation
Uncontrolled rare item values
Constraint 5 — Accessibility

New players must have:

Clear progression
Beginner weapons
Learning systems
