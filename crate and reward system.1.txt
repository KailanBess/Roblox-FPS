221. KEY SYSTEM

All crates require keys.

Crates cannot be opened directly with premium currency.

Key Sources

Keys are obtained from:

Events

Examples:

Seasonal events
Community goals
Limited challenges
Login Rewards

Daily:

Small key chances

Weekly:

Guaranteed keys

Monthly:

Large key rewards

Workshop Achievements

Examples:

Publish approved creation
Gain reputation milestones
Player Level Rewards

Major levels provide keys.

Purchases

Players may purchase key bundles.
