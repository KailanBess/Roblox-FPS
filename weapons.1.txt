75. WEAPON ECOSYSTEM OVERVIEW

Weapons are the core gameplay identity of the FPS ecosystem.

The weapon system is designed around:

Accessibility
Mastery
Personal preference
Competitive balance
Long-term progression

Players should develop relationships with weapons.

A weapon should not only be:

"Something you equip."

It should become:

"Something you master."

75.1 Weapon Categories

The ecosystem contains:

System Weapons

Developer-created weapons.

Purpose:

Stable competitive foundation
Consistent balance
Ranked reliability
Workshop Weapons

Player-created weapons.

Purpose:

Creativity
Community contribution
Endless content expansion
76. SYSTEM WEAPON PHILOSOPHY

System weapons provide the baseline experience.

They must:

Be balanced
Be readable
Have clear roles
Remain competitive forever

System weapons should represent:

Beginner accessibility
Competitive options
Mastery potential

Not:

Create barriers.
