24. HEALTH SYSTEM

Base player health:

MAX_HP = 200

Health represents the player's actual life pool.

Armor does not increase health.

24.1 Health Rules

Health:

Regenerates only through approved systems
Cannot exceed 200
Is separate from armor
24.2 Healing Rules

Healing sources:

Map pickups (if enabled)
Game modes
Pet abilities
Special mechanics

Healing cannot:

Increase max HP
Ignore combat balance
25. ARMOR SYSTEM

Armor is a damage reduction layer.

Armor does not:

Add HP
Extend TTK through extra health

Instead:

Armor reduces incoming damage.

25.1 Armor Constants
MAX_ARMOR = 150

MAX_ARMOR_DAMAGE_REDUCTION = 30%
25.2 Armor Reduction Formula

Formula:

Armor Reduction =

(Armor / 150) × 30%

Examples:

150 Armor

=
30% damage reduction
75 Armor

=
15% damage reduction
0 Armor

=
0% damage reduction
25.3 Armor Damage Formula

Final damage:

Final Damage =

Base Damage

× Upgrade Modifier

× Headshot Modifier

× Movement Modifier

× (1 - Armor Reduction)

Example:

Weapon damage:

40

Enemy armor:

150

Reduction:

30%

Calculation:

40 × 0.70

=

28 Damage
26. ARMOR PENETRATION

Armor penetration reduces the effectiveness of armor.

It does not increase raw damage.

26.1 Armor Pen Formula

Example:

Weapon:

Armor Pen = 70

Enemy armor:

150

Effective armor:

150 - 70

=

80 Armor
26.2 Purpose

Armor penetration creates weapon identity.

Examples:

AK-103:

High armor penetration.

Purpose:

Strong against armored players

FAMAS:

Lower penetration.

Purpose:

High DPS against lightly armored targets

30. TIME TO KILL SYSTEM

Formula:

TTK =
