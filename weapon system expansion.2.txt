435. WEAPON BALANCE OVERVIEW

The weapon ecosystem is built around predictable competitive balance.

Every weapon must have:

A clear role
A specific combat range
A skill requirement
Counterplay options
435.1 BALANCE PHILOSOPHY

Weapons are balanced through:

Time To Kill

Primary metric.

Ease Of Use

Secondary metric.

Combat Range

Role identity.

Mobility

Positioning strength.

437. DAMAGE STAT

Controls:

Base damage per shot.

Affected by:

Headshots
Armor
Enhancements

Damage cannot exceed:

Class balance limits.

438. FIRE RATE STAT

Measured:

RPM

Higher RPM:

Advantages:

Faster TTK
Better pressure

Weakness:

Lower per-shot damage
More ammo consumption
439. MAGAZINE STAT

Controls:

Rounds before reload.

Higher magazine:

Advantage:

Longer fights.

Weakness:

Usually:

Lower reload speed
Lower mobility
440. RANGE STAT

Controls:

Damage consistency over distance.

Higher range:

Means:

Slower damage falloff
Better long-range performance
441. ACCURACY STAT

Controls:

Spread
Bloom
Crosshair size

Formula:

Crosshair Size = 136 - Accuracy
442. RELOAD STAT

Controls:

Time between magazines.

Important for:

Aggressive players
Sustained fights
443. MOBILITY STAT

Controls:

Movement while equipped.

Higher mobility:

Advantage:

Positioning

Weakness:

Usually:

Lower control
444. ARMOR PENETRATION STAT

Controls effectiveness against armor.

Does not ignore armor.

It reduces armor protection.

445. TTK BALANCE FRAMEWORK

Every weapon is evaluated using:

Shots To Kill

×

Fire Interval

Formula:

Shots To Kill =
Ceiling(Target HP / Damage)
TTK =
(Shots To Kill - 1)
×

(60 / RPM)
