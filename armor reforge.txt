🛡️ Reforge Eligibility

All armor pieces can be reforged:

Helmet
Chestpiece
Backpiece
Leggings
Gloves
Shoes

Each armor piece contains:

1 Weapon-Specific Bonus
+
1 Universal Bonus

Both stats are eligible for rerolling.

💰 Standard Reforge

Rerolls BOTH armor affixes.

Cost:

2,000 Coins

OR

90 Gems

Result:

Weapon Bonus = Randomized

Universal Bonus = Randomized

Both previous stats are replaced.

🔒 Stat Lock Reforge

Players may choose to keep ONE existing stat.

Locked stats cannot change during the reforge.

Cost:

4,000 Coins

OR

180 Gems

Result:

Locked Stat = Preserved

Unlocked Stat = Randomized
Example

Current Helmet:

SMG Accuracy

Universal Reload Speed

Player locks:

SMG Accuracy

Reforge result:

SMG Accuracy

Universal Movement Speed

Only the unlocked stat changes.

🎲 Duplicate Validation

Every reforge must obey the armor affix rules.

Server validates:

Maximum 2 identical Weapon Bonuses

Maximum 2 identical Universal Bonuses

Invalid rolls are automatically rerolled.

Example:

Already have:

AR Damage
AR Damage

A third AR Damage roll cannot occur.

🏆 Reforge Preview System

Before confirming, players see:

Current Bonuses

New Bonuses

Cost

Locked Stat Indicator

Buttons:

Accept

Decline

Players are never forced to accept a reroll.

♻️ Scrap Refund System

Destroyed armor grants Scrap.

Example:

Common = 5 Scrap

Uncommon = 10 Scrap

Rare = 20 Scrap

Epic = 40 Scrap

Legendary = 80 Scrap

Mythic = 150 Scrap

Scrap can later support future crafting systems.

🔒 Competitive Safeguards

Reforging cannot:

Increase Damage Beyond Armor Caps

Break TTK Limits

Ignore Duplicate Rules

Create Invalid Affix Combinations

Exceed Ranked Stat Limits

The system remains progression-focused rather than pay-to-win.
