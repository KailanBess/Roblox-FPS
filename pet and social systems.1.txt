269. PET SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Pets are a companion customization system.

Pets provide:

Personality
Cosmetic identity
Small passive gameplay assistance

Pets are not combat replacements.

They do not:

Attack enemies
Reveal enemies
Provide unfair information
Increase weapon power directly
269.1 PET DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Pets should feel like:

"An assistant that supports my playstyle."

Not:

"A second player helping me win."
