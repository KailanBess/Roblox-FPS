157. ENHANCEMENT SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Enhancements are controlled weapon modifiers designed to increase player choice without creating overwhelming power differences.

Enhancements allow players to specialize weapons toward different playstyles.

Examples:

A player may customize an assault rifle into:

Faster handling
More consistent accuracy
Better magazine efficiency

However:

Enhancements cannot transform a weapon into a completely different class.

157.1 ENHANCEMENT PHILOSOPHY

Enhancements follow:

Choice Over Power

The purpose is:

"How do I want to use this weapon?"

Not:

"How do I make this weapon objectively stronger?"

166. ENHANCEMENT BALANCE RULES

Enhancements must follow:

Rule 1

No enhancement may reduce TTK beyond balance limits.

Rule 2

Every advantage requires a weakness.

Rule 3

Enhancements cannot change weapon class identity.

Example:

A shotgun cannot become a sniper.

Rule 4

Stacking identical advantages is limited.

167. ENHANCEMENT STACKING RULES

Weapons cannot equip:

Multiple offensive enhancements.

Example:

Not allowed:

Damage enhancement

Damage enhancement

Reason:

Prevents extreme builds.
