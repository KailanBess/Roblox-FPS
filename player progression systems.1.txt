174. PLAYER PROGRESSION OVERVIEW

Player progression represents overall account growth.

It rewards:

Time invested
Skill improvement
Participation
Exploration of systems

Player progression does not increase combat power.

174.1 PROGRESSION PHILOSOPHY

Progression should answer:

"What have I accomplished?"

Not:

"How much stronger am I than new players?"
